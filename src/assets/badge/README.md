# Corner badge photos

Drop photos in this folder and they show up in the rotating badge (bottom-left of the spiral). No code change needed.

- Landscape 16:9 works best (about 560x315 px), WebP or JPG (png works too). Square or portrait photos are cropped to fit.
- Photos play in file-name order: `badge-1.webp`, `badge-2.webp`, ... (use `badge-10` only after renaming the others to `badge-01` etc.).
- The tile hangs off the bottom-left corner of the screen, so only its top-right part shows: keep the face in the upper right of the photo.
- Delete the three placeholders when you add your own. With an empty folder the badge shows a plain grey tile.
