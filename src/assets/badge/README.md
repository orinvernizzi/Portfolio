# Corner badge photos

Drop photos in this folder and they show up in the corner badge (bottom-left of the spiral) and in the photo reel that opens when you press it. No code change needed.

- Any aspect ratio works for the reel: landscape, portrait or square, each photo is shown whole at its own shape. About 1600 px on the long side is plenty (WebP or JPG, png works too), ideally under ~160 kB each so the reel opens fast.
- Photos play in file-name order: `badge-1.webp`, `badge-2.webp`, ... (use `badge-10` only after renaming the others to `badge-01` etc.).
- The small tile is cropped to 16:9 and hangs off the bottom-left corner, so only its top-right part shows: keep the subject (face) in the upper area of the photo, towards the right.
- Delete the three placeholders when you add your own. With an empty folder the badge shows a plain grey tile and pressing it does nothing. With one photo, the reel shows just that photo.
